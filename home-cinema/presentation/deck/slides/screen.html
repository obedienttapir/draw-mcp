Movie night: a 110″ drop-down screen

A motorised, tab-tensioned screen hides in a pocket in the alcove bulkhead, 9″ in front of the TV.

A ceiling projector sits about 11′ back (for a 1.4 throw lens).

The TV stays for news, sports and gaming.

Movie scene: pots off, LEDs at 18%, screen down.

Throw distance depends on the projector model; check its throw calculator before setting the ceiling box. The screen case needs a framed pocket in the bulkhead, sized to the model you pick, with power and a 12 V trigger wire from the projector or receiver.
