Why the fireplace wall reads as a face

Two equal windows side by side, with a wide fireplace centred below, give the eye two eyes and a mouth.

Tall, jagged flames read as teeth, and the LED edges frame it all like a head.

The openings are already cut, so every fix keeps them: join the two eyes into one shape, hide them, or break the symmetry.

This is pareidolia: we see faces in anything with two similar shapes over a wider one, especially when it is symmetrical and framed. Nothing is wrong with the materials; it is only the arrangement of three rectangles.
