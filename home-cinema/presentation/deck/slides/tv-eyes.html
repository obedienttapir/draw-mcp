The TV wall has a face too

Before · two pots over the TV

After · cove light only, 85″ TV

The two pots in front of the bulkhead paint two bright scallops above the screen, and they glare on it during movies.

Drop them, or use adjustable pots aimed at the angled side walls. The LED cove and a bias light do the rest.

If the two pot boxes are already installed, swap the trims for adjustable gimbal trims and aim them at the angled walls, so there are no matching scallops centred over the TV. Put them on their own dimmer zone so Movie can switch them off.
