Three more looks for the same wall

C · Hidden + stone

Slats and black felt close over both windows, and a tall stone panel frames the fire. No eyes left at all.

D · Black band + art

A black band wall to wall turns the windows into two slots in one stripe. Art leaned off-centre breaks the symmetry.

E · Linen shade + shelves

One shade covers both windows as a single soft panel; shelves either side widen the fireplace into a built-in wall.

All five looks are one click in the planner: Finishes › Fireplace wall looks.

Every look keeps the openings as cut. C and D need privacy film or tinted glass so no light leaks from the room next door. E works on a painted wall, without the slats. For the picture ledge in D, check the fireplace's clearance to combustibles above the opening.
