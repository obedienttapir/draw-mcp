Fix B: one black frame and a ledge

A 2″ black steel surround goes around both windows, and the strip between them is clad black. The pair reads as one long window.

A floating walnut ledge between the windows and the fireplace separates the eyes from the mouth.

A hides the windows; B shows them off as one window. Three more looks follow.

Fix B keeps the windows fully visible and is simpler: the slats stop around one rectangle instead of running across the glass, and there is no LED band to wire. Fix A is the stronger look at night.
