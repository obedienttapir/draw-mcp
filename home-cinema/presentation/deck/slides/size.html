How big should the picture be?

From the prime seat the screen is 13′-6″ away. Cinema guidance asks the picture to fill at least 30° of view (SMPTE), ideally 36–40° (THX).

110″ drop-down screen96″35°Yes, hangs 9″ off the wall

Recommended: the 85″ TV for every day, and the 110″ screen for movie nights. Angles measured in the planner from the sectional’s centre seat.

The 98-inch TV would give 30 degrees but is 1.5 inches wider than the flat TV wall, so it would overlap the angled corners. The drop-down screen hangs 9 inches in front of the TV wall, where the alcove is already about 102 inches wide, so a 110-inch screen fits.
