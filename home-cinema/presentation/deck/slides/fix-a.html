After · recommended

Slats continue over both windows. A 2700K strip behind them, along the window line, makes one band of light.

A floating walnut bench under the fireplace adds perch seating and a strong horizontal line.

Set the flame effect low and even. Tall, spiky flames read as teeth.

The windows still borrow light from the room next door; the slats just stop them reading as two separate shapes. Leave the slats unglued over the windows (screwed from the back of a removable panel) so the glass can still be cleaned.
