Step lights for movie nights

Low-glare LED step lights in the stair wall, one per tread.

With the pots off, the stairs are the darkest route in the room.

On in Movie, Evening and Game night; off in Bright.

The boxes go in the stair wall before drywall, so they belong on the pre-wire list. Pick lights that aim down at the tread and do not shine toward the sectional.
