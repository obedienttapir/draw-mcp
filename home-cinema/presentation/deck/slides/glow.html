A bias light behind the TV

A 6500K LED strip on the back of the TV lights the wall around it.

In a dark room it eases eye strain and makes blacks look deeper.

Keep it neutral white; the warm 2700K is for the room, not the screen.

Game night scene: TV and fire on, LEDs at 80%, pots at 25%.

Bias light kits made for TVs are rated around 6500K to match the D65 white point video is mastered to. Set it to roughly 10 percent of the screen's brightness.
